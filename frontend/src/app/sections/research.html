Reproducing Pratama et al. (2024)

The paper's 97.7% only appears when test data leaks into training

We implemented the paper's exact pipeline (feature merging, 6 engineered features, median imputation, one-hot encoding, StandardScaler, 80/20 split, LR with max_iter=1000, default Random Forest, per-department evaluation). With a correct split the scores are far lower. Oversampling leavers before splitting — so copies of the same employee land in both train and test — reproduces the paper's near-perfect Random Forest numbers (a memorising model). It does not reproduce the paper's Logistic Regression figures (a linear model cannot memorise duplicates), so those remain unexplained.
